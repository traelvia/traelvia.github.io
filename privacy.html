Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Traelvia, the trail-running game for iPhone and iPad (the “app”), and this website handle information. Questions can be sent to traelvia@gmail.com.

In short
  • Traelvia does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • There is no Traelvia account and no developer-operated game server.
  • Game progress is stored on your device.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and Game Center activity.

1. Data stored by the app

Traelvia stores your runner name and appearance, settings, course records, run history, unlocked content, in-game currency, inventory and purchase-delivery identifiers locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game and preserve progress.

You can reset game progress from the app. Some purchase-related records may be retained locally so that paid currency or content is not accidentally delivered twice. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to backups and services controlled by Apple.

2. App Store purchases

Optional course access and in-game currency are offered through Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. The app receives product and transaction information needed to unlock or deliver the purchase and prevent duplicate delivery. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

3. Game Center

If you use Game Center, Traelvia may submit eligible scores and achievements and request leaderboard information through Apple’s GameKit service. Your Game Center identity and related activity are managed by Apple. You can control Game Center in your device settings. Core gameplay remains available without Game Center authentication.

4. iCloud and device backups

The current version does not operate its own cloud-sync service. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your iCloud and backup settings. If a future Traelvia version adds optional iCloud game-data sync, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

5. Data the developer collects

Traelvia does not send gameplay, profile, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or diagnostic analytics to the developer. The developer receives personal information only when you choose to contact support, such as your email address, message and any diagnostic details or attachments you voluntarily provide.

Support correspondence is used to answer the request, troubleshoot the app and maintain necessary support records. Do not send sensitive personal information.

6. This website

This static website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details to operate and secure its hosting service under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

7. Children

Traelvia is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated server, the developer does not knowingly collect children’s gameplay data. A parent or guardian can contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information by email.

8. Retention, access and deletion

Local game data remains until you reset progress or delete the app. Apple-held purchase, Game Center and backup data is controlled through your Apple Account and device settings. Support email is retained only as reasonably needed to answer the request, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to us, email traelvia@gmail.com.

9. Security and international processing

Local data is protected by the safeguards of your device. Email and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. The policy that applies is the version published when the relevant handling occurs.

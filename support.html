Trail support

How can we help?

Traelvia is built for iPhone and iPad and keeps its core game available offline. Try the steps below, or send us a message with the details of what happened.

Email support
Usually the best route for a game or purchase issue.traelvia@gmail.com

Common questions

A purchase did not unlock

Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase and has an internet connection. Open the Shop and use Restore Purchases for course packs or the All Mountain Pass. Consumable cairn packs are delivered from their StoreKit transaction and are not restored like non-consumable products. If a completed charge was not delivered, include the product, approximate purchase time and App Store receipt status in your email—never send full payment-card details.

Game Center scores are missing

Check that Game Center is signed in under Settings on your device, then reopen Traelvia while online. Local records still work while signed out. Scores that do not meet a leaderboard’s current rules may remain local.

I changed or lost my device

The current version stores game progress locally. Restore from an Apple device backup where available. Non-consumable course access can be restored from the Shop with the same Apple Account; local run history and consumable balances may not return without a device backup.

The game has no sound or haptics

Check Music, Volume and Haptics in Traelvia Settings. Also check the device volume, Silent Mode, Focus settings and whether audio is routed to headphones or another device.

How do I reset my runner?

Use the reset option in Traelvia Settings. Read the confirmation carefully: runner and record data can be removed, while purchase safeguards and paid value may be preserved so purchases are not delivered twice.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, Traelvia version, course and game mode, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it. A screenshot is helpful. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

App Store billing and refunds

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. For charges or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase-support tools. We can help identify the Traelvia product involved, but we cannot access your payment method.

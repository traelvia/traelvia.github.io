Trail support

How can we help?

Traelvia is built for iPhone, iPad and Mac and keeps its core game available offline. Try the steps below, or send us a message with the details of what happened.

Email support
Usually the best route for a game or purchase issue.traelvia@gmail.com

Common questions

Does Traelvia run on Mac?

Yes. Traelvia is a native Mac app (macOS 15 or later) and the same purchase as on iPhone and iPad: course packs you own open on Mac too, and with iCloud your runner, records, cairns and kit follow you between your devices. Hold the mouse or trackpad button, or the Space bar, to push. Esc pauses, closes windows and goes back; Return presses the main button; drag lists with the mouse to scroll them. The game plays in one landscape window that you can resize or make full screen, and Settings is in the Traelvia menu (⌘,).

A purchase did not unlock

Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase (on Mac, in the App Store app) and has an internet connection. Select a course of the pack, or the Endless traverse for the All Mountain Pass, and use Restore Purchases in its panel; a pack bought on iPhone or iPad unlocks on Mac the same way. Consumable cairn packs are delivered from their StoreKit transaction and are not restored like non-consumable products. If a completed charge was not delivered, include the product, approximate purchase time and App Store receipt status in your email—never send full payment-card details.

Game Center scores are missing

Check that Game Center is signed in under Settings on your device, then reopen Traelvia while online. Local records still work while signed out. Scores that do not meet a leaderboard’s current rules may remain local.

I changed or lost my device

With iCloud turned on for Traelvia, your runner, records, stars, achievements, Endless tables, cairns, kit and supplies come back on a new iPhone, iPad or Mac signed in to the same Apple Account; sound, language and units stay per device. Without iCloud, progress lives on the device and returns only from a device backup. Course packs can always be restored with the same Apple Account (Restore Purchases in a pack’s course panel).

The game has no sound or haptics

Check Music, Volume and Haptics in Traelvia Settings. Also check the device volume, Silent Mode, Focus settings and whether audio is routed to headphones or another device. A Mac has no haptics for games, so Settings shows them as unavailable there.

How do I reset my runner?

Use the reset option in Traelvia Settings. Read the confirmation carefully: runner and record data can be removed, while purchase safeguards and paid value may be preserved so purchases are not delivered twice.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include your iPhone, iPad or Mac model, iOS/iPadOS/macOS version, Traelvia version, course and game mode, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it. A screenshot is helpful. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

App Store billing and refunds

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. For charges or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase-support tools. We can help identify the Traelvia product involved, but we cannot access your payment method.

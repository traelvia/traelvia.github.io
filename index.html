One thumb or one key · Real pacing

Simple to touch.
Hard to master.

The runner moves forward. You decide when to spend effort and when to breathe. Steep grades drain stamina, descents reward patience, and the breath line tells you how far you can push before the legs give out. On Mac, hold the mouse or trackpad button, or the Space bar.
